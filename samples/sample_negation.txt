Snapfolio Privacy Policy

What we collect
We collect your email address, username, the photos you upload, and your approximate location while the app is open.

How we use it
We use this information to run the app, recommend content, and prevent abuse.

Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. We never share your precise location with advertisers. We may share aggregated, de-identified usage data with analytics providers.

Your choices
You can download or delete your data at any time from Settings, then Privacy.

How long we keep it
We keep your account data until you delete your account, and for up to 30 days afterward so you can change your mind.
